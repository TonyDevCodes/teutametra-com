About The Author

I'm an Albanian-Dutch novelist based in Rotterdam, the Netherlands. In my books, I focus not only on women's lives but also on the interconnectedness of different cultures which transcends borders and mentalities.

As a former journalist, I deeply believe in the impact of words, both in literature and in everyday life. Alongside my work as a librarian in Rotterdam, I support non-native speakers in gaining confidence in English and Dutch and in finding their way in a new culture. I speak Albanian, English, Dutch and Italian.
